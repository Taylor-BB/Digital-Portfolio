# ArcMap Sample

This page contains a final project that was completed for the University of Central Florida’s (UCF) “GIS for Environmental Studies (GIS 3043C)” course. As reference, the primary goal of this final project was to identify a real-world challenge that could be directly addressed through GIS (vector-based) spatial analysis. For Taylor’s final project, she chose to identify optimal locations for a new relief (EMS or hospital) location on Sanibel Island, Florida. Through the analysis of relevant environmental and social phenomena in Esri's ArcMap, she identified 17 locations that could support a new relief (EMS or hospital) location on Sanibel Island, Florida.
