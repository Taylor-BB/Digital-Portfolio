# AutoCAD Sample

This page contains documents that were completed for the University of Central Florida’s (UCF) “AutoCAD Basics” continuing education certification program. As reference, the primary goal of this program was to expose students to the features and functions of Autodesk’s AutoCAD program through the development of various architectural, electrical, structural, and other drawings.
