# Technical Presentation Sample

This page contains a final project that was completed for the University of Central Florida’s (UCF) “Introduction to Sustainable Design (EVR 3733)” course. As reference, the primary goal of this project was for students to develop their understanding of global sustainability efforts. Specifically, students were tasked to research and present the demographics and unique sustainability-focused design elements of a region. For Taylor’s final project, she chose to research and present the demographics and sustainable transportation, decarbonization, and ecological designs of Osaka, Japan utilizing Microsoft’s PowerPoint program.
