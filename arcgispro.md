# ArcGIS Pro Sample

This page contains a final project that was completed for the University of Central Florida’s (UCF) “Advanced GIS Applications in Environmental Studies (GIS 4301C)” course. As reference, the primary goal of this final project was to identify a real-world challenge that could be directly addressed through GIS (raster-based) spatial analysis. For Taylor’s final project, she chose to identify optimal locations for a new community garden in Orange County, Florida. Through the analysis of relevant environmental and social phenomena in Esri's ArcGIS Pro, she identified two possible locations that could best support a new community garden in Orange County, Florida.
