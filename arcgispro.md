# ArcGIS Pro Sample

This page contains a final project that was completed for the University of Central Florida’s (UCF) “Advanced GIS Applications in Environmental Studies (GIS 4301C)” course. As reference, the primary goal of this final project was to identify a real-world challenge that could be directly addressed through GIS (raster-based) spatial analysis. For Taylor’s final project, she chose to identify optimal locations for a new community garden in Orange County, Florida. Through the analysis of relevant environmental and social phenomena in Esri's ArcGIS Pro program, she identified two possible locations that could best support a new community garden in Orange County, Florida.

This page also contains a final project that was completed for the University of Central Florida’s (UCF) “Information Systems for Public Managers and Planners (PAD 4712)” course. As reference, the primary goal of this final project was to identify optimal locations for a new solar energy research park in Osceola, Florida through GIS (vector-based) spatial analysis. Through the analysis of relevant environmental and social phenomena in Esri’s ArcGIS Pro program, she recommended two possible locations that met all of the site's listed requirements.
