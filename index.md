# Taylor Beebe

Taylor Beebe is a Master of Science student at the University of Central Florida (UCF). As a UCF Master of Science in Urban and Regional Planning student, and UCF Bachelor of Science in Environmental Studies alum, she has formed a deep passion for sustainable, resilient, and equitable environmental planning. To further develop her interdisciplinary understanding of urban planning and environmental regulation, she pursued UCF’s Geographic Information Systems (GIS) undergraduate certification, AutoCAD Basics continuing education certification, and OSHA’s Certified Environmental Specialist certification. Throughout her education, she developed skills in various industry-leading software including Esri’s ArcGIS Pro, Autodesk’s AutoCAD, Trimble’s SketchUp, and Microsoft’s Word, PowerPoint, Excel, Outlook, Teams, and Access. Beyond this, Taylor is an avid learner outside the classroom, with previous student-based professional experience from Palm Beach County’s Planning Division and Environmental Resources Management department. The work samples provided in her portfolio help to illustrate her expertise in environmental planning.
